# Introduction
Hey there! I'm Aryan Goodarzi from Iran. I'm a Pixel Art and Blockbench , and I'm also a lifelong learner, diving into all 
kinds of knowledge. Plus, I'm on a coding journey, eager to pick up as many programming languages as I can.


### The Story

---

Ever since I can remember, I've had a huge passion for computers. I'd sit in front of my monitor, completely absorbed 
in figuring things out. My mom loves to share this story about how, at just 4 years old, I managed to open up our home 
computer and load up games.Jokes on you I was even a big fan of real-time strategy games. However, my 
real love for coding didn't really kick in until my parents signed me in a creative academy in Iran when I was still in 
3rd grade. The academy's goal was to explore kids interests and passions. During the final exams, the professor suggested 
I had a good talent for robotics, and he wasn't wrong. I always dreamed of building robots, and he recommended that my 
family sign me to a coding academy to start learning as soon as possible, but life had other plans for me.

By the time I turned 15, I had left Iran, which opened up a whole new chapter in my life. At that age, I managed to 
download a pirated version of Minecraft Pocket Edition, and that changed everything for me. I jumped into this franchise 
just as it was experiencing a revolution, with new add-ons and mods being introduced through third-party sources and 
the marketplace. Even though I was eager to create my own add-ons, I never got around to it. However, I did dive into 
some research and managed to dismantle the Minecraft app on my Samsung Galaxy Tab 4, which was my first taste of 
coding when I worked with a JSON file and tried to understand how things worked.

Eventually, I started playing around with XML, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. But since I was going through a tough time in 
my life, I never really got to fully explore or learn these languages. I just left it all behind, and that was pretty 
much it until around 2024 when I had this amazing idea to create my own game, which is what brought me here.
